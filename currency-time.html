How it works

The converter takes the official Central Bank of Russia rates for the latest available date and for the day you pick in the past. The amount is converted to rubles first, then to the currency you need, just like in regular currency converters.

The Central Bank sets rates on business days. If you pick a weekend or a holiday, the last published rate is used. The history covers about five years.

The main cards show exchange rate changes only. The “Inflation-adjusted” card (for the ruble, dollar and euro) also accounts for price growth in both countries; inflation data is updated monthly, and the ruble index is derived from the Bank of Russia’s annual inflation figures, so it is approximate. Bank fees and the gap between the Central Bank rate and exchange office rates are not included.

Formula: amount × (rubles per source currency on the date) ÷ (rubles per target currency on the date).
