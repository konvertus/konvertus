Payment type
How the payment types differ

Annuity — the same payment every month. At the start most of each payment is interest and the debt shrinks slowly, so total interest is higher. Good if you want a steady budget and a smaller first payment.

Differentiated — the principal is split into equal parts and interest is charged on the remaining balance. Payments fall every month: the first is the largest, the last the smallest. Total interest is lower, but because of the large first payments a bank may ask for a higher income.
