What is interest capitalization

Capitalization means the interest you have earned is added to the deposit, and from then on interest is calculated on the larger amount: “interest on interest”. Without capitalization the interest builds up separately and is paid at the end of the term, and it earns nothing itself.

How the calculator works. The annual rate is divided by 12, and each month interest is calculated on the current deposit amount. With monthly capitalization it is added to the deposit at once, with quarterly capitalization every 3 months, with yearly capitalization every 12 months.

Example. ₽100,000 at 12% for a year. Without capitalization the interest is ₽12,000. With monthly capitalization: 100,000 × (1 + 0.12 / 12)12 ≈ ₽112,683, so the interest is ₽12,683. The more often interest is capitalized and the longer the term, the bigger the difference, which is why the effective rate is higher than the nominal one.

Refillable deposit. If you tick the box, the calculator adds a top-up at the end of every month except the last. A top-up starts earning from the next month: on a one-year deposit, a top-up in month 4 works for 8 months. Top-ups earn interest too, and with capitalization that interest earns interest as well.
