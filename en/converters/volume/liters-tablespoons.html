Liters to tablespoons

Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Liters to gallons divide by 4 0.264172
Gallons to liters multiply by 4 3.785412
Cups to milliliters multiply by 240 236.5882
Tablespoons to milliliters multiply by 15 14.78676
Teaspoons to milliliters multiply by 5 4.928922
Fluid ounces to milliliters multiply by 30 29.57353

General formula: value × (liters in the source unit) ÷ (liters in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to liters first, then to the unit you need. Unless stated otherwise, gallons, quarts, pints, cups, ounces and spoons here are US units.

Interesting facts

Liter

It is the volume of a cube with 10 cm sides, exactly 1 dm³. One liter holds 1,000 ml and 1,000 cm³.

Gallon

A US gallon is 3.785 L and a British one is 4.546 L. Because of this, “miles per gallon” figures in the US and the UK cannot be compared directly.

Cup

A US cup is about 236.6 ml, though recipes often round it to 240 ml. In Australia and some other countries the metric cup is 250 ml.

Tablespoon

A US tablespoon is about 14.8 ml and equals three teaspoons.

Fluid ounce

A US fluid ounce is about 29.57 ml, and a gallon holds 128 of them. The British one is slightly smaller: 28.41 ml.

Cubic meter

It holds 1,000 liters. An Olympic swimming pool contains about 2,500 m³, or 2.5 million liters of water.
