Km/h to knots

Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Km/h to knots divide by 1.85 0.539957
Knots to km/h multiply by 1.85 1.852
Knots to m/s about half 0.514444
Knots to miles per hour add 15% 1.150779
Km/h to miles per hour multiply by 0.6 0.621371

General formula: value × (m/s in the source unit) ÷ (m/s in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to meters per second first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

What a knot is

One knot is one nautical mile per hour, or 1.852 km/h. It is the standard unit of speed at sea and in aviation.

The nautical mile

It equals 1,852 meters and roughly matches one arcminute along a meridian, which is why sailors find it handy on charts.

Where the name comes from

Ship speed used to be measured with a log line: a rope with knots thrown overboard. Crews counted how many knots ran out in a set time.

Wind in knots

Marine and aviation forecasts often give wind speed in knots too: 10 knots is about 18.5 km/h.

No “knots per hour”

A knot already means “nautical mile per hour”, so saying “knots per hour” would be like saying “miles per hour per hour”.

Sailing record

In 2012 the sailboat Vestas Sailrocket 2 reached about 65 knots, roughly 121 km/h.
