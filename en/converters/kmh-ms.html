Km/h to m/s

Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Km/h to m/s divide by 3.6 0.277778
M/s to km/h multiply by 3.6 3.6
Miles/h to km/h multiply by 1.6 1.609344
Km/h to miles/h multiply by 0.6 0.621371
Knots to km/h multiply by 1.85 1.852

General formula: value × (m/s in the source unit) ÷ (m/s in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to meters per second first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

Meter per second

It is the SI unit of speed. A handy anchor: 10 m/s is exactly 36 km/h, and 1 m/s is 3.6 km/h.

Knot

One knot is one nautical mile per hour, or 1.852 km/h. The name comes from a rope with knots that sailors let out behind the ship to measure its speed.

Speed limit signs

In the USA and the UK roads use miles per hour: a 60 mph limit is about 97 km/h.

A sprinter

At his peak in the 100 m world record in 2009, Usain Bolt ran at about 44 km/h, which is 12.4 m/s.

Space station

The International Space Station flies at about 7.66 km/s, which is roughly 27,600 km/h. It circles the Earth about every 90 minutes.

Speed of light

Light travels at exactly 299,792,458 m/s, which is almost 1.08 billion km/h. It is the same number that defines the meter.
