Km/h to m/s

Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Km/h to m/s divide by 3.6 0.277778
M/s to km/h multiply by 3.6 3.6
Miles per hour to km/h multiply by 1.6 1.609344
Km/h to miles per hour multiply by 0.6 0.621371
Knots to km/h multiply by 1.85 1.852

General formula: value × (m/s in the source unit) ÷ (m/s in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to meters per second first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

Meter per second

It is the SI unit of speed. To get km/h, multiply by 3.6: exactly 10 m/s is 36 km/h.

Knot

One knot is one nautical mile per hour, or 1.852 km/h. The name comes from the age of sail, when speed was measured with a knotted rope thrown overboard.

Miles per hour

Road signs in the US and the UK show speed in miles per hour. A 60 mph limit is about 97 km/h.

Mach number

Mach 1 is the speed of sound. Near the ground it is about 340 m/s, or roughly 1,225 km/h, and it depends on the air temperature.

Speed of light

299,792,458 m/s is an exact value: since 1983 the meter has been defined through it. In km/h that is about 1.08 billion.

Land speed record

In 1997 the car ThrustSSC reached 1,228 km/h and became the first land vehicle to break the sound barrier.
