How to convert by hand

The converter above does it instantly. If you want to do it by hand, these rules help:

Conversion How to calculate Example
Binary to decimal add up the powers of two where there is a 1 1011 = 8 + 2 + 1 = 11
Decimal to binary divide by 2 and write the remainders from bottom to top 13 → 1101
Binary to hexadecimal split into groups of 4 bits from the right 1111 0000 = F0
Binary to octal split into groups of 3 bits from the right 101 110 = 56
Hexadecimal to decimal digit × 16 to the power of its position 2A = 2 × 16 + 10 = 42

How the converter works: it first turns your number into an ordinary integer, then writes it in the system you need. Only whole numbers are supported, but the length is almost unlimited. You can type the letters A–F in any case.

Interesting facts

Binary system

Gottfried Leibniz described it in detail in 1703. It suits computers because a transistor has two states: on and off.

Hexadecimal

This is how colors are written on websites. For example, this site’s accent color #ab5e4b is red 171, green 94 and blue 75.

Octal

File permissions in Linux are set with three octal digits. The famous chmod 755 is three groups of three bits.

Sexagesimal

The Babylonians counted in base 60. That is why there are 60 minutes in an hour and 360 degrees in a circle.

Byte and 255

Eight bits give 256 values, from 0 to 255. That’s why in IP addresses such as 192.168.0.1 no number is larger than 255.

Ten digits aren’t a law

The decimal system feels familiar, but there is nothing “natural” about it. Some peoples counted in fives or twenties.
