Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Hectares to square meters multiply by 10,000 10,000
Ares (sotka) to square meters multiply by 100 100
Hectares to acres multiply by 2.5 2.471054
Acres to hectares multiply by 0.4 0.404686
Square meters to square feet multiply by 10.8 10.76391
Square miles to square kilometers multiply by 2.6 2.589988

General formula: value × (square meters in the source unit) ÷ (square meters in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to square meters first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

Hectare

It is a square with 100-meter sides. Fields and forests are measured in hectares: 1 ha is 10,000 m², or 100 ares.

Ares (sotka)

In everyday Russian an are is called a “sotka”: 100 m², a square of 10 × 10 meters. Dacha plots are usually measured this way: six sotkas is 600 m².

Acre

An acre was once the plot a team of oxen could plow in a day. Today it is 4,046.86 m², about 0.4 hectares.

Square mile

It holds exactly 640 acres and is roughly 2.59 km². Land area in the US is counted in acres and square miles.

Square foot

In the US and the UK, homes and offices are measured in square feet. A 50 m² apartment is about 538 ft².

The smallest state

Vatican City covers about 0.44 km², just 44 hectares. It is the smallest country in the world.
