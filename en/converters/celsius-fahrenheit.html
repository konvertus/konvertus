Celsius to Fahrenheit

Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Celsius to Fahrenheit double it and add 30 × 1.8 + 32
Fahrenheit to Celsius subtract 30 and halve it (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8
Celsius to Kelvin add 273 + 273.15
Kelvin to Celsius subtract 273 − 273.15
Celsius to Réaumur multiply by 0.8 × 0.8

Why it’s not just multiplication: the scales have different zero points, so the length and weight converters won’t work here. Our converter first turns the value into degrees Celsius, then from Celsius into the scale you need.

Interesting facts

Celsius

In 1742 Anders Celsius made his scale upside down: 0° was the boiling point of water and 100° was the freezing point. The order was reversed later.

Fahrenheit

Zero on the Fahrenheit scale is the temperature of a mix of water, ice and salt. Water freezes at 32 °F and boils at 212 °F.

Kelvin

0 K is absolute zero, which is −273.15 °C. Nothing can be colder. The word “degree” isn’t used for kelvins: you just say “300 kelvins”.

Rankine

The Rankine scale works like kelvins, but its step is the size of a Fahrenheit degree. It is used in some American engineering calculations.

Réaumur

In Russia the Réaumur scale was used until the early 20th century: water boils at 80 °Re. You can still meet it in old books.

−40°

This is the only point where the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales show the same number: −40 °C = −40 °F.
