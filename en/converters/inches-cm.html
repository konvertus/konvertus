Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Miles to kilometersmultiply by 1.61.609344
Kilometers to milesmultiply by 0.60.621371
Inches to centimetersmultiply by 2.52.54
Feet to metersmultiply by 0.30.3048

General formula: value × (meters in the source unit) ÷ (meters in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to meters first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

Meter

Since 1983 the meter has been defined through the speed of light: it is the distance light travels in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second.

Inch

Today an inch is exactly 25.4 mm. This was agreed in 1959 so that it would not differ between countries.

Nautical mile

It is 1,852 meters, which is one minute of latitude on the Earth’s surface. That makes it handy for navigation.

Mile

The word comes from the Latin “mille passus”, a thousand paces. This is how Roman legionaries measured distances.

Nanometer

A human hair is about 80,000 nanometers thick. That is still hundreds of thousands of times larger than an atom.

The cost of a mistake

In 1999 NASA lost the Mars Climate Orbiter: one team worked in metric units and another in imperial.
