Milligrams to micrograms

Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Pounds to kilograms multiply by 0.45 (about half) 0.45359237
Kilograms to pounds multiply by 2.2 2.204623
Ounces to grams multiply by 28 28.349523
Grams to ounces divide by 28 0.035274
Stones to kilograms multiply by 6.35 6.350293

General formula: value × (kilograms in the source unit) ÷ (kilograms in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to kilograms first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

Kilogram

Since 2019 the kilogram has been defined through the Planck constant. Before that, for almost 130 years, it was a platinum-iridium cylinder kept near Paris.

Pound

Today a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg. This was fixed in 1959 so that the US and Commonwealth countries would use the same value.

Ounce

A regular ounce is about 28.35 g, while the troy ounce used for gold is about 31.1 g. Be careful not to mix them up.

Carat

One carat is 200 mg. The word comes from the seeds of the carob tree, which were once used to weigh gemstones.

Pood

The Russian pood is about 16.38 kg. It went out of use after the switch to the metric system in the 1920s.

Stone

In the UK a person’s weight is still often given in stones: one stone is 14 pounds, or about 6.35 kg.
