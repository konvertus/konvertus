Easy mental math

The converter above gives the exact result. For a quick estimate, these rules are enough:

Conversion In your head Exact
Megabits to megabytesdivide by 8÷ 8
Megabytes to gigabytesdivide by 1000÷ 1000
Gigabytes to terabytesdivide by 1000÷ 1000
Mebibytes to megabytesmultiply by 1.051.048576
Gibibytes to gigabytesmultiply by 1.071.073742

General formula: value × (bytes in the source unit) ÷ (bytes in the target unit). This is how our converter works too: everything is converted to bytes first, then to the unit you need.

Interesting facts

Bit

The name is short for “binary digit”. It appeared in the late 1940s and became established after the work of Claude Shannon.

Byte

The word was coined by IBM engineer Werner Buchholz in 1956. Eight bits to a byte became the standard later, with the mass-market computers of the 1960s.

Why your disk looks “smaller”

A 1 TB drive holds 10¹² bytes. Windows divides that by 2³⁰ and shows about 931 “GB”, although these are really gibibytes.

Kibibytes

The prefixes kibi, mebi and gibi appeared in the late 1990s to tell 1024 apart from 1000. People still mix up the usual “kilobytes”.

Megabits vs megabytes

Internet plans are quoted in megabits per second, while downloads show megabytes. 100 Mbit/s is about 12.5 MB/s.

Petabyte

It is a million gigabytes. That is roughly a thousand 1 TB hard drives.
