How to read the table

Bank buys is the rate at which the bank takes currency from you when you sell it. Bank sells is the rate at which you buy currency from the bank. The difference is the spread, which is the price of the exchange: the smaller it is, the better.

Choose what you want to do and the banks line up from the best to the worst offer. The last column shows how many rubles you pay or get for the amount you entered.
