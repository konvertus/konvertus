About us

Konvertus is a small website for quickly converting one unit into another. No ads, no tracking.

Our principles

No ads

There are no banners, pop-ups or sponsored links here. The site exists so you can do a calculation, not so we can sell you something.

No data collection

The site has no counters, analytics or cookies. The numbers you enter are never sent anywhere: all calculations happen in your browser.

No sign-up

Every converter works right away, with no account and no email confirmation.

What this project is

Konvertus is a final project in web development. We built it to bring together everything we have learned: layout, styling, JavaScript and working with external data.

This is a student project, so some things may be unfinished. If you find a mistake or want a new converter, write to us.

How it’s built

The site is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks or build tools. The conversion factors come from official definitions: for example, an inch is exactly 25.4 mm and a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg.

Currency rates come from the Bank of Russia. They are for reference only: banks and exchange offices use different rates, so check your own bank’s rate for a real transaction.

What about your data, really

Konvertus itself collects and stores nothing about you. The only thing kept on your device is the last downloaded set of currency rates: it sits in your browser’s storage so it can be shown if the connection drops.

An honest caveat: fonts and icons are loaded from Google’s servers, and currency rates from cbr-xml-daily.ru. As with any website, these services can see your IP address and the technical details of the request. Konvertus does not receive that data.
