Privacy

Without signing in

If you do not sign in, the site stores nothing about you. The numbers you enter in converters and calculators are processed in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

What is stored when you sign in

Your email address and account ID (stored by Google Firebase Authentication). If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address of your Google account.

Statistics and recommendations

Only if you turn on consent in the account window, we count which converters and calculators you use: how many times you opened a category or a unit pair and when you were last active. The numbers you enter are not stored. These counters are used to show you recommended links to offers. The data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore).

Opting out and deleting your data

You can withdraw consent in the account window at any time, and counting stops. The same window has a “Delete account and data” button that erases the counters and the account itself.

Contact details are on the About page.
